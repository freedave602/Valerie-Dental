Questions

Good to know

Tap a question to open the answer.

Does treatment hurt?

We use local anaesthetic and take it slowly. Tell us at any point and we pause.

Can I bring my child?

Yes. Children are welcome from their first tooth, and checkups are quick and friendly.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel any time from My Appointments using your email and date of birth so the slot goes to someone else.

How do I pay?

Cash and mobile money are accepted at reception. A quote is given before any treatment.
