Privacy & your data

Plain-English notes on what we keep and why.

What we collect

Your name, contact details, date of birth, address and the medical history you choose to share, plus your appointments and any messages you send us.

Why we collect it

To register you as a patient, book and manage your treatment, and contact you about your care. We do not use your details for advertising or sell them.

How we protect it

Details are stored in a protected database. Staff reports need a login, staff passwords are stored as one-way hashes, and every database request uses prepared statements.

Your rights

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) you can ask to see, correct or delete your information. Email hello@valeriadental.example and we will respond promptly.

Accessibility

We build to WCAG 2.1 AA: keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, labelled forms and reduced-motion support.
